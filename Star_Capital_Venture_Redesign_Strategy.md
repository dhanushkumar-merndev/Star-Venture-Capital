# MASTER PROMPT — STAR VENTURE CAPITAL WEBSITE

## Your role

Act as a senior product designer, brand designer, UX writer, and web developer. Build a complete, responsive website for Star Venture Capital.

Before implementation, inspect the available project files, existing codebase, brand assets, and any current Star Capital page or website materials available in the project. Reuse verified assets and established project conventions where appropriate. Do not assume that the current Star Groups website or its codebase is available if it is not present.

## Project goal

Create a distinct web presence for Star Venture Capital. Visitors should quickly understand:

1. What Star Venture Capital is.
2. What it looks for in founders and companies.
3. How its connection to Star Groups may be relevant.
4. How to start an enquiry.

The Star Groups website may link to Star Venture Capital, but this page must have its own clear identity, original copy, and dedicated experience. Do not simply reproduce the Star Groups homepage or reuse its general company copy.

## Design direction

Create an original, premium, founder-facing design.

- Use a white or warm-white hero and make light surfaces dominant throughout the page.
- Use black or near-black for typography and Star red for deliberate accents.
- The hero must use a new composition created for this page: an asymmetric editorial layout with a clear copy area, generous whitespace, and a custom visual anchor related to growth, building, or connected capabilities.
- Do not use a black or near-black hero, a full-bleed dark hero, or the previous currency-symbol / GradientASCII hero concept.
- Do not make DepthText or another supplied effect component the central design concept.
- Avoid generic finance dashboards, stock handshake imagery, invented portfolio-company imagery, and AI-generated founder portraits.
- Keep animation restrained and decorative. The page must work as a complete static experience and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Maintain accessible contrast, semantic headings, keyboard access, responsive layouts, and clear focus states.

Use Ascent Capital only as a reference for placing verified firm facts, investment sectors, and portfolio proof in a clear sequence. Use StartupXseed only as a reference for discoverable navigation, portfolio grouping, and team information architecture. Do not copy either firm’s wording, imagery, metrics, portfolio, team information, or visual identity.

Reference websites:
- https://www.ascentcapital.in/
- https://startupxseed.in/

## Brand and business information available

The known Star Groups businesses and broad capabilities are:

- StarTech India — software and business workflows, including CRM, HRMS, ERP, websites, and automation.
- Star Growth Hub — branding and digital marketing.
- Star Production — video and content production.
- Star Garden — landscaping, plants, vertical gardens, and maintenance.
- Star Space Interiors — interior design.

These can be shown as a Star Groups capability map. Do not claim that every capability is included in Star Venture Capital’s investment offer, is free, is guaranteed, or is automatically available to portfolio companies. Explain this relationship carefully and use wording that can be edited after business confirmation.

Do not invent Star Capital’s investment stage, sectors, geography, cheque size, portfolio, returns, fund size, team, track record, legal status, or investment process. Treat any such details found in project files or existing web content as unverified until clearly supported by an approved source.

## Page structure and draft content

Build one complete page with the following sections in this order.

### 1. Header

- Star Venture Capital identity.
- A subtle “A Star Groups company” relationship line.
- Navigation: Approach, Investment Focus, Star Groups Capabilities, Portfolio, Team, Contact.
- Primary action: “Founder Enquiry”.
- Keep the header light and readable. On mobile, use an accessible menu and keep the enquiry action easy to reach.

### 2. Hero

Use this as draft copy:

Eyebrow:
“STAR VENTURE CAPITAL · A STAR GROUPS COMPANY”

Headline:
“Build what’s next.”

Supporting copy:
“We back ambitious founders building businesses with the potential to grow and endure.”

Primary CTA:
“Start a conversation”

Secondary CTA:
“Explore our approach”

Place the copy in a clear text column and create a custom visual anchor beside it. The visual should feel specific to Star Venture Capital and its relationship to Star Groups. It may express connection, capability, or business growth through original abstract geometry or a simple network of labeled capabilities.

Treat the supporting copy as editable draft language. Do not add claims about funding stage, cheque size, outcomes, or guaranteed support.

### 3. At a glance

Create a compact facts section for approved investment details, such as:

- Investment stage
- Sectors
- Geography
- Investment model

Only display fields supported by approved information. If a field is unconfirmed, omit it from the published view rather than displaying a guessed value or a public-facing “TBC”.

### 4. Investment approach

Draft heading:
“Capital, paired with a considered approach.”

Provide a short explanation of Star Capital’s actual investment philosophy. Use business-approved language if available. If it is not available, keep the section structure ready for final copy and do not invent a thesis.

### 5. Investment focus

Present approved investment stages, sectors, and company-fit criteria as a clear grid or editorial list. Use specific labels and concise descriptions. Do not label Star Capital “sector agnostic” or assign sectors without confirmation.

### 6. Star Groups capabilities

Show the five known group businesses as a visual capability map:

- StarTech India — Software and business workflows
- Star Growth Hub — Branding and digital marketing
- Star Production — Video and content production
- Star Garden — Landscaping and plant services
- Star Space Interiors — Interior design

Use copy that describes these as capabilities within the wider Star Groups ecosystem. State or imply portfolio access only to the extent confirmed by the business. Do not imply guaranteed, free, or bundled services.

### 7. Portfolio

If approved portfolio-company information exists in the project materials, create a portfolio section using only those entries. Each entry may include:

- Company name and approved logo
- Sector
- One-line factual description
- A link to the company, if supplied and verified

If the portfolio can accurately be grouped as All, Active, and Exits, add those filters. Otherwise, do not invent status categories. If no portfolio information is approved, omit the section or replace it with an approved alternative. Never fabricate portfolio companies or investment examples.

### 8. Investment process

Include a process section only if a real process is documented in the available approved material. Do not invent stages, response times, review periods, or outcomes. If no process is confirmed, leave this section out.

### 9. Team

Show team members only when approved names, roles, bios, and images are available. Do not create fictional profiles or generic executive portraits. If information is unavailable, omit the section.

### 10. Founder enquiry

Heading:
“Let’s start a conversation.”

Create an accessible enquiry form with these proposed fields:

- Founder name
- Company name
- Email
- Website
- Sector
- Funding stage
- Short introduction

Use appropriate labels, validation, success and error states, and spam protection if supported by the existing project. Do not invent a destination email or submit form data to an unverified endpoint. If the enquiry destination is unavailable, build the form UI and leave its integration clearly configurable.

### 11. Footer

Include:

- Star Venture Capital name and approved contact details.
- A clear relationship to Star Groups.
- Links to verified company pages.
- Privacy and legal links only if supplied.
- Required investment or regulatory disclosures only if supplied and approved.

Do not invent an address, email, legal entity, registration, or disclaimer.

## Implementation requirements

- Follow the project’s existing framework, routing, styling, and component conventions if a codebase is provided.
- If no codebase is provided, create a clean, responsive implementation using an appropriate modern web stack.
- Keep page copy and business facts in a structured, easy-to-edit location rather than scattering them through components.
- Make the layout work on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Implement navigation links, CTA behavior, form states, and portfolio filters only when their data exists.
- Use supplied brand assets; do not create replacement logos.
- Use optimized images and avoid unnecessary animation or heavy effects.
- Make sure the page remains understandable if motion, external imagery, or decorative effects fail.

## Final review

Before delivery:

1. Check that the hero is light and visibly distinct from a generic VC template.
2. Confirm that no black hero or currency-symbol/GradientASCII effect remains.
3. Confirm every business claim is supported by project material or explicitly treated as editable draft copy.
4. Check that the Star Groups capability map does not promise unconfirmed portfolio access.
5. Check responsive behavior, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
6. Summarize what was implemented and list any business details that still need confirmation.
