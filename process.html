Investment Process

A transparent, respectful founder evaluation.

We respect your time. Our investment workflow is structured to provide clarity at every checkpoint without drawn-out ambiguity.

01
Within 5 Business Days

Initial Pitch & Alignment Review

We review your submitted materials with an emphasis on problem depth, team moat, and defensible market dynamics. If there is mutual promise, we initiate an introductory partner call.

02
1 to 2 Weeks

Deep-Dive & Ecosystem Fit

An exploratory working session delving into product architecture, unit economics, and how Star Capital and Star Groups strategic relationships could accelerate your trajectory.

03
1 to 2 Weeks

Collaborative Diligence & Term Sheet

Frictionless, founder-friendly due diligence covering financials, customer references, and cap table structure, leading to a clean and straightforward term sheet.

04
Immediate Deployment

Syndicate Close & Partnership

Capital transfer completed promptly upon legal closing. Onboarding includes tailored access to ecosystem capability advisors and ongoing board-level advocacy.

Founder FAQ

Common questions about our process.

Everything you need to know about how we partner, review submissions, and manage term sheets.

What materials do I need to submit to get started?

You only need to complete our short Founder Portal form with your company details and a link to your current pitch deck or executive summary. We do not require complex financial models at the initial introductory stage.

Do you lead rounds or co-invest?

We do both. We frequently anchor rounds for seed companies where our Star Groups capabilities offer distinct strategic tailwinds, and we also participate as a collaborative value-add syndicate partner alongside institutional lead venture funds.

Are Star Groups capabilities mandatory or bundled into the investment?

No. Portfolio companies retain complete autonomy. Any operational collaboration with Star Groups businesses (such as StarTech India or Star Growth Hub) is completely voluntary, strictly based on mutual strategic alignment, and independently agreed upon.

How does Star Venture Capital maintain confidentiality?

All materials submitted through our Founder Portal are reviewed exclusively by our Investment Committee partners under strict fiduciary and institutional non-disclosure standards.
