Evaluation Criteria

What we evaluate in founding teams.

We look beyond pitch decks to evaluate the fundamentals that allow a venture to scale through market cycles.

Dimension 01 Founder Fit

Customer Obsession

Founders with deep domain empathy who understand customer pain points at an intuitive, granular level and move rapidly on customer feedback.

Dimension 02 Moat

Defensible Edge

Clear structural moats—whether through proprietary data assets, network effects, high switching costs, or unique ecosystem integration.

Dimension 03 Economics

Capital Discipline

A clear path to sustainable unit economics, prudent burn rates, and resilience against volatile macro funding environments.

Dimension 04 Synergy

Ecosystem Acceleration

Venture models that can uniquely benefit from optional strategic collaboration with Star Groups companies in tech, media, and built spaces.
